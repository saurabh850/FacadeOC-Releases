## Key Features

### Flawless Long-Term Memory
Characters dynamically extract and store details from conversations to create seamless, long-lasting relationships. Your choices and their quirks are tracked with mechanical precision.

### Infinite Roster & Universal Importer
Build an unlimited collection of diverse characters. The intuitive roster system lets you seamlessly organize, search, and jump back into ongoing narratives with any character at a moment's notice. Transitioning your favorite characters is instant: simply drop a character card into the engine and FacadeOC automatically parses and integrates their personality and background.

### Multiple Player Personas
Craft unique identities for yourself. Whether you want to be a seasoned adventurer in one story or a curious scholar in another, you can effortlessly switch between profiles to match the narrative perfectly.

### Complete Customization
Set the mood for every conversation with your own custom background images. Upload any artwork, photograph, or aesthetic to fully personalize the atmosphere of each story. Adjust everything from text generation parameters to UI themes in real-time.

### Developer Mode for Power Users
Take full control under the hood. Unlock granular controls over the AI's generation parameters, samplers, and repetition penalties to fine-tune exactly how characters respond.

### 100% Offline & Private
Every story you tell stays on your hard drive. Once the initial model is downloaded, FacadeOC runs completely offline. No telemetry, no logs, and no cloud processing. Your data is yours.

- **OS:** Windows (10/11), macOS, or Linux

## FAQ

**Is this a subscription?**
Absolutely not. FacadeOC is a one-time purchase. You will never be charged again. There are no API costs, no monthly fees, no usage limits. 

**Do I need an internet connection to play?**
Only once during the initial model download. After that, FacadeOC runs 100% offline.

**What if I lose my download files?**
No problem, your download link is permanent. Check the purchase receipt email for your license key and re-download link.
